# VoiceScheme - 3-Minute Demo Video Recording Script
### Scaler x Wispr Flow Hackathon ("Build for Bharat") | Round 2 Submission
**Video Length:** ~2 Minutes 30 Seconds (strictly under 3 minutes)  
**Tools to record:** Windows Snipping Tool (Win + Shift + R) / OBS Studio / Loom / Chrome Screen Recorder

---

## 🎬 Video Recording Plan & Timeline

```
0:00 - 0:30  | Introduction: The Problem & The Mission of VoiceScheme
0:30 - 1:15  | Live Demo 1: Farmer Scenario (English) - Voice input, speech reply, scheme cards
1:15 - 1:55  | Live Demo 2: Regional Indic Language (Hindi/Telugu) - Widows/Artisans
1:55 - 2:15  | Architecture & Hackathon Compliance: Zero API keys, Docker Compose, 300+ lines code
2:15 - 2:30  | Conclusion & Finale Vision
```

---

## 🎙️ Step-by-Step Script

### [0:00 - 0:30] Hook & Problem Introduction
* **Screen:** Show the VoiceScheme web app running cleanly at `http://localhost:5000` (or Docker terminal).
* **What you say:**
  > *"Namaste! In India, over 300 million citizens—small farmers, elderly widows, and rural artisans—miss out on life-saving government welfare schemes worth crores of rupees simply because they cannot read complex English forms or navigate bureaucratic portals.*
  > 
  > *Today, I am proud to present **VoiceScheme**, a voice-first welfare discovery platform built for the Scaler x Wispr Flow Hackathon. With VoiceScheme, citizens don't need to type or fill forms—they simply speak their situation in their mother tongue, and VoiceScheme speaks back with the exact schemes they are entitled to."*

---

### [0:30 - 1:15] Demo 1: Marginal Farmer Crop Loss (English Voice Query)
* **Screen:** Click the pulsing microphone button (or click the Marginal Farmer chip).
* **What you speak into the microphone (or chip):**
  > *"I am a small farmer with 2 acres land in Karnataka, and my paddy crop was damaged by rains. What government schemes can help me?"*
* **Screen shows:**
  1. Live voice transcript appearing instantly in the transcript box.
  2. Spoken voice reply plays aloud: *"Namaste! Based on what you shared, you qualify for 2 key government welfare schemes..."*
  3. Dynamic cards render: **PM Fasal Bima Yojana** (Crop insurance compensation) and **PM-Kisan** (₹6,000/yr direct cash transfer).
  4. Highlight the **Eligibility Checklist** and **Documents Required** (Aadhaar, Land Record, Bank Passbook).
* **What you say:**
  > *"Notice how within seconds, VoiceScheme extracted the occupation, land size, and distress factor, and matched them directly against official criteria. A farmer can even click 'Listen' on any card to hear the details read aloud."*

---

### [1:15 - 1:55] Demo 2: Multilingual Indic Dialogue (Hindi or Telugu)
* **Screen:** Switch language to **हिंदी (Hindi)** or **తెలుగు (Telugu)**.
* **Click mic or the sample chip:**
  > *"मेरे पति का देहांत हो गया है, मेरे दो छोटे बच्चे हैं और घर कच्चा है। मुझे कौन सी सरकारी पेंशन और आवास योजना मिलेगी?"*
* **Screen shows:**
  1. Voice reply box plays Hindi audio: *"नमस्ते! आपके विवरण के अनुसार आप मुख्य सरकारी योजनाओं के पात्र हैं..."*
  2. Scheme cards update in real time showing **Indira Gandhi Widow Pension (इंदिरा गांधी विधवा पेंशन)** and **PM Awas Yojana (प्रधानमंत्री आवास योजना)** with required documents clearly listed.
* **What you say:**
  > *"VoiceScheme seamlessly understands regional Indic vocabulary without requiring external translation APIs. It speaks back with empathy, ensuring no citizen is left behind due to illiteracy."*

---

### [1:55 - 2:15] Architecture & Hackathon Compliance
* **Screen:** Show the terminal running `docker compose up` and the clean project structure.
* **What you say:**
  > *"Technically, VoiceScheme is built completely from scratch for Round 2, exceeding 350+ lines of custom Python and JavaScript code. Most importantly, it requires **zero secret API keys** to run—judges can start the entire project with a single command: `docker compose up`. Our offline deterministic NLP engine guarantees 100% reliability with zero downtime or token costs."*

---

### [2:15 - 2:30] Conclusion & Call to Action
* **Screen:** Back to the clean VoiceScheme main page.
* **What you say:**
  > *"VoiceScheme transforms civic welfare from an intimidating bureaucratic maze into an empowering, 45-second voice conversation. Thank you to Scaler and Wispr Flow for inspiring us to Build for Bharat!"*

## 💡 Quick Tips Before Recording
1. Ensure your browser microphone permission is allowed for `localhost:5000`.
2. Keep your computer volume up so the screen recorder captures the audio response from the assistant.
3. Keep the video between 2:15 and 2:45 minutes.
4. Upload to YouTube as **Public** or **Unlisted** and paste the link in the hackathon submission form!
